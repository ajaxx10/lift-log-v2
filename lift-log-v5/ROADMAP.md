# Lift Log Roadmap

## Completed through v5.0.0

- Three core athletic-strength workout types and optional fourth day
- Bench-priority 24-week progression
- Custom Other-exercise blocks
- Gym-first logging, prior-session context, and one-tap reuse of prior sets
- Adaptive progression with increase / repeat / deload decisions and explanations
- Automatic rest timer, dark mode, and offline PWA
- Goal roadmap, coaching dashboard, estimated 1RM trends, weekly volume, PR tracking, and strength score
- Exercise-level history pages
- Bodyweight tracking
- Backward-compatible local data storage and backup/restore

## Potential v6

- Calendar scheduling and planned workout dates
- Supersets and exercise reordering
- Relative-strength scoring using bodyweight
- Recovery/sleep inputs that influence recommendations
- Travel-workout substitutions
- Optional cloud sync and authentication
